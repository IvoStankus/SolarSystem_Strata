About & scale
Sizes and distances are compressed (radii ∝ real radius0.5, orbit radius ∝ AU0.62) so everything stays visible on one screen. Orbital shapes, eccentricities, tilts, orbital directions, spin directions and moon ordering are real; moons ride at exaggerated distances. The asteroid belt sits between Mars and Jupiter, holding ~1,500 rocks; the real belt's mass is only about 4% of the Moon's.
